# Changelog policy

Preserve the existing Keep a Changelog-style layout: meaningful entries go under `## [Unreleased]`, use an existing category where appropriate, and begin with a `YYYY-MM-DD:` date. Do not invent release numbers or dates.

Add a concise feature-level entry for meaningful completed functionality, user-visible behavior changes, important fixes, breaking changes, architecture changes with developer-facing impact, or significant tooling/workflow changes. Do not add entries for every code, config, asset, or documentation edit; skip formatting, trivial cleanup, superseded intermediate work, and unfinished scaffolding with no usable capability.

Before writing, inspect current implementation and behavior, relevant tests, and history/context when needed. Confirm the capability is implemented, not merely proposed or superseded, and describe the final result accurately. Keep existing historical entries untouched unless asked to correct them; group a capability logically rather than adding one entry per commit.
